# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-04

First playable foundation (Phases 1–4, part of 5).

- Game engine: 3 races × 2 genders × 3 evolutions, 6 attributes (3 points/level), 18 race skills +
  2 set skills, 48 item templates, 5 rarities, random bonuses, upgrades, 2 sets, deterministic combat.
- Server: auth, pets, builds, equipment, upgrades, salvage, evolution, zones with capped passive
  income, async PvP with rating matchmaking + anti-farming, battle storage + verification,
  discovery missions, ledger, audit log, rate limits, idempotency.
- Client: mobile-first PWA with hand-drawn SVG pets, animated battle replays, battle report.
- 62 tests, balance simulator.
